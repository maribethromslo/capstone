THE WHY

“We celebrate birthdays, graduations, and championships. Why don’t we celebrate the courage it takes to navigate illness?”

THE DESIGN QUESTION

How might design help people feel seen in the experiences that medical records don’t capture?

A project informed by lived experience.

01 / CONTEXT

The problem

Illness can become the primary lens through which people are seen. Medical records document diagnoses, treatments, and outcomes. The personal milestones and everyday work of living with illness often remain invisible.

This project explores how a visual identity can acknowledge those experiences while making room for the whole person.

02 / OPPORTUNITY

A different kind
of field guide

What if healthcare borrowed from the language of scouting, national parks, and field guides to recognize the journeys people experience?

Badges of Honor brings that familiar camp vocabulary into an adult experience of chronic illness: something tangible to hold, wear, collect, or make your own.
